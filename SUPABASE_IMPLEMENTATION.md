## Accepted assumptions and decisions

- Website only; keep the existing design and shared service boundary.
- Browsing remains authenticated. Non-draft events (including cancelled/closed history) remain visible, matching the original UI; drafts are private to their organization/admins.
- Roles are student, organizer and globally privileged admin. Each test admin has an organization affiliation, but admin authority is global. Existing organizer screens serve admins across organizations; no separate dashboard or profile editor was invented.
- Preserve separate tickets and version-1 QR identity fields; scanning remains simulated. No signing, expiry or check-in window was invented.
- Preserve organizer draft-only editing, event cancellation retention and deduplicated check-in. Database constraints apply even to privileged workflows where necessary for history/capacity.
- Cancellation retains one registration and ticket, releases its seat and prevents check-in. The same student cannot re-register that event; checked-in registrations cannot be cancelled.
- No waitlist, payment, announcements or custom forms. Program/year/student ID, organization description/logo and optional end/deadline/banner fields are nullable because existing forms do not collect them.
- Categories and artwork themes remain the existing controlled presentation options. Spotlight event references, date and venue now come from data.
- Demo global reset is replaced with an explanatory test-data control; shared data cannot be wiped from the website.
- No browser-local data import, email registration notification, camera scanner, Realtime/offline synchronization, or production deployment was added. Auth email confirmation follows hosted settings.
- Test-only records are retained as evidence. Seeding is explicit and must not run against a production project or during an active campaign.
